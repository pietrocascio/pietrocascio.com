# Course promotion

The editorial and campaign vocabulary for content that advertises Piero's Pluralsight courses — a recurring content pillar distinct from the [engineering-essay domain](../../CONTEXT.md), written to give a course more visibility without teaching its content.

## Language

**Teaser**:
A single LinkedIn post built around one course module's storyline hook or pain point, designed to leave the reader curious rather than informed.
_Avoid_: promo post, ad, update.

**Spoiler boundary**:
The line between what a teaser may reveal (storyline, pain points, one-line rules of thumb, screenshots of slides or broken/setup code) and what it must withhold (working solution code, decision-matrix or checklist clips, full demo walkthroughs), so the course itself remains the only place to learn the fix.
_Avoid_: giving too much away, spoilers.

**Capstone post**:
The blog article published on pietrocascio.com after a course's teaser series wraps, linking back to the full series and to the course itself.
_Avoid_: recap post, summary article.

**Campaign**:
The full set of teasers plus its capstone post for one course, run at a weekly cadence with no fixed start date.
_Avoid_: series, push.
