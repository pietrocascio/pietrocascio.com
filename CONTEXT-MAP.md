# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Engineering essays](./CONTEXT.md): editorial voice and vocabulary for architecture/technical-debt/team-dynamics essays, written for senior engineers and tech leads
- [Course promotion](./src/courses/CONTEXT.md): vocabulary and rules for content that teases Piero's Pluralsight courses, written for entry-level learners

## Relationships

- Engineering essays and Course promotion are independent domains with no shared vocabulary — deliberately kept separate because they target different audiences (senior engineers vs. entry-level learners) and serve different purposes (reflective analysis vs. curiosity-driven marketing).
